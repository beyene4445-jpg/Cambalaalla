✨ "Fichee" = the sacred stick • "Cambalaalla" = transition & renewal. Celebrated with unity, forgiveness, and the shared buurisame feast.

📅 Sidama Calendar

🔬 Modern Science Link

The Sidama used the Pleiades (Kore) star cluster and lunar phases to fix Fichee — a method matching modern sidereal astronomy used by NASA & ESA today.

🎉 Celebration

Families gather, elders bless the community, and everyone shares buurisame. It marks forgiveness, peace, and the start of a fresh year.
